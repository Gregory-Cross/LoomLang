PROPOSED What an evaluated symbol adds to a row is read from its whole checked action and not from its body alone. PROPOSED Every definition actually invoked in admission or result checking, including literal, constructor-field and membership helpers and their transitive callees, must meet EV-121's current conservative state-free eligibility constraint whether marked or unmarked. A safe outer helper does not excuse a forbidden inner call. Passing that effect predicate is neither totality nor successful execution nor a full admission proof. A check run has its own fuel/evidence boundary under EV-124; the preliminary admission/checker/object phase and resource interface is still incomplete. No free checking or chosen start-size restriction follows.

If the test consumes an argument which the operation then uses, it must hand that value back; otherwise refuse the guard. This local handback condition is not a replacement for complete use-once checking. Test, condition and failure remain in the row with roles/times and in the call's costs. A test remaining at a call is a use; a tracking claim moving it inside owes the whole guarded-call behavior.

PROPOSED A yes condition alone does not license deleting what its test observes, chooses or may fail to terminate doing. Machine-theory rule10 marking/evidence remains the source's separate obligation, not a new mark chosen here.
