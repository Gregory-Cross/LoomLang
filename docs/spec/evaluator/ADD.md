**PROPOSED · paired reference rule.** A start fixes the state before an occurrence. A grant is its available work and construction pair (W,C). A hold reserves credit privately; a commit publishes the result, paired debit and mandatory record together. An observer selects the public fields of a run. ## Price and transition

Fix two obtained naturals x,y in their admitted order and exact invoice arithmetic. Let bits count binary digits, with bits(0)=1. Read a=bits(x) and b=bits(y) from their validated headers. Put m=max(a,b), p=bits(2(a+b+2)), R=m+1 and T=4p. The work invoice is w=a+b+R+T+2(m+1). The construction invoice is c=bits(x+y). These prices cover the selected operation after its operands are obtained.

1. If W<w, work is the primary stop. Use only the two headers to compute S={a+1} for a=b≥2, and S={m,m+1} otherwise. Record the work deficit w−W and construction deficit image D={max(0,s−C) for s in S}. Mark sufficient if max D=0, short if min D>0, and undetermined otherwise. Keep min D and the unevaluated expression max(0,bits(x+y)−C), bound to descriptor, row, reached event, both ordered immutable references and effective C. A singleton D certifies its deficit. The size pass is unreached, the prefix is preserved and the event debits neither axis.
2. If W≥w, hold work and perform the exact read-only size pass, even at C=0. If C<c, retain the measured c and deficit c−C, release the hold and stop for construction. Both debits are zero.
3. If both prices fit, reserve the full pair and stage privately. At the specified interruption before commit, sample the live reservation and phase, discard staging and release the hold. Preserve the previous prefix. Otherwise atomically commit the natural x+y, its exact paired debit and record.
