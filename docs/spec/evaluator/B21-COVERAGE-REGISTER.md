## How to read the register

A *start* fixes the offered state, grant, scope and intervention schedule. An *observer* selects the facts visible at an export. A *producer* is the implementation that emitted the record at the reached transition. An *edition* binds exact source bytes. A *separator* is the first case chosen to distinguish competing readings. A *receipt* binds an actual result to its subject and inputs.
**J** checks a judgment on fixed premises with an independent derivation and changed-premise/conclusion controls. **E** checks an actual outside event emission with separate trace comparison and witness decoding at the same start, descriptor, grant and observer. **X** requires independently identified real builds, compiler, host and observations at the slice boundary. Passing a component check cannot establish its composition.
**Disposition.** Included identifies the scope under review, not a pass. Excluded names the relevant authority and boundary. Unresolved retains a pending decision, price, source, artifact or threshold. NOT RUN means the named evidence is absent. Actual receipts and controls are SOURCE when bound; appointments, new inputs and control designs are PROPOSED. Negative controls that correctly reject are successful checks; failed, missing, incomplete or capped evidence remains visible at its original scope.
**Edition convention.** DES and FORM denote the exact editions in the source table. Unless a different descriptor is identified, an open row has no bound numerical execution profile. A J result on supplied premises cannot choose the missing tariff. Proposed owners name evidence responsibilities, not dispatches; Claude A resolves staffing, exposure and ordering. The owner decides changes of standing, publication, profile adoption or phase authority.
